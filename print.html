Student at Millersville University

For my print project, we were assigned to create a bookmark for Eshleman Elementary. I designed a rocket ship bookmark, which was both fun and educational for the kids. We utilized the printing machines to mass produce these bookmarks, ensuring each one was consistent and high-quality. This project not only allowed me to explore creative design but also provided hands-on experience with professional printing equipment.
